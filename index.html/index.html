🧭

TCP vs Base Coordinate Frames

Understand how homogenous transformation matrices shift rotation and translation from the robot's physical mounting pedestal (Base) to the active cutter tip (TCP).

FUNDAMENTAL LEVEL
⚡

MoveJ (Joint) vs MoveL (Linear)

MoveJ moves each motor along its quickest articular path, minimizing wrist strain. MoveL forces the TCP along a straight Euclidean vector, critical for cutting and dispensing.

PRACTICAL APPLICATION
🛡️

ISO/TS 15066 Cobot Safety

Biomechanical limit calculation, clamping force sensors, and SIL-3 dual bus safety interlocks when cobots share workspaces with operators.
